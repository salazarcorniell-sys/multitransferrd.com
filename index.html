Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a transfer from Punta Cana Airport (PUJ) cost?+

Transfers from Punta Cana Airport start at $39 USD to Bávaro/Cap Cana, $49 to Uvero Alto, $79 to La Romana/Bayahibe, and up to $310 for Puerto Plata. Per vehicle, up to 6 passengers — no hidden fees.

How much is a transfer from Santo Domingo Airport (SDQ)?+

From SDQ: $35 to Boca Chica, $45 to Santo Domingo city or Juan Dolio, $79 to La Romana/Bayahibe, $129 to Punta Cana. Private vehicle, up to 6 passengers.

Does MultiTransfer serve La Romana Airport (LRM)?+

Yes! We serve La Romana International Airport (LRM / Casa de Campo). Transfers start at $30 to Bayahibe, $75 to Punta Cana/Bávaro, and $99 to Santo Domingo.

Is the service available 24 hours a day?+

Yes. MultiTransfer operates 24/7, 365 days a year, including holidays. We monitor your flight in real time and wait at no extra cost if delayed.

Which airports does MultiTransfer serve?+

We operate from all 5 airports: Punta Cana (PUJ), Las Américas - Santo Domingo (SDQ), Cibao - Santiago (STI), Gregorio Luperón - Puerto Plata (POP), and La Romana / Casa de Campo (LRM).
